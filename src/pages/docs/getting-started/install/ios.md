# iOS Installation

Dasher is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.

## Downloading from the App Store

**[Download Dasher on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/dasher)**

### System Requirements

- iOS 12.0 or later
- Compatible with iPhone and iPad
- Optimized for iPad Pro with Apple Pencil support

## Installation

1. Open the **App Store** on your iPhone or iPad
2. Search for "Dasher"
3. Tap **Get** or the download icon
4. Wait for installation to complete
5. Launch Dasher from your home screen

## Supported Devices

Dasher for iOS works on:

- **iPhone** (iOS 12.0 or later recommended)
- **iPad** (iOS 12.0 or later recommended)
- **iPad Pro** with Apple Pencil support

## Input Methods on iOS

### Touch (All Devices)

- Navigate by dragging your finger across the screen
- Supports multi-touch gestures

### Apple Pencil (iPad Pro)

- Use Apple Pencil for precise navigation
- Pressure sensitivity for speed control

### Switch Access

- Compatible with iOS Switch Control
- Use external switches or the screen as a switch
- Configure in iOS Accessibility settings

## First Run

1. Open Dasher from your home screen
2. Grant accessibility permissions if prompted
3. Select your alphabet/language from Settings
4. Choose your preferred input method
5. Adjust speed and sensitivity settings

## Configuring iOS Switch Control

To use external switches with Dasher:

1. Go to **iOS Settings → Accessibility → Switch Control**
2. Enable **Switch Control**
3. Add your switches under **Switches**
4. Configure scanning settings
5. Open Dasher and select "Switch" as input method

## VoiceOver Integration

Dasher is designed to work alongside VoiceOver:

- VoiceOver can read text as you enter it
- Use rotor gestures for navigation
- Configure audio feedback in Dasher settings

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** App crashes on launch

**Solution:**

- Ensure iOS 12.0 or later
- Restart your device
- Check for iOS updates
- Reinstall the app from the App Store
- Check for app updates in the App Store

**Problem:** Can't enter special characters

**Solution:**

- Some alphabets group special characters separately
- Navigate through the zooming interface to find them
- Check that your alphabet file includes needed characters
- Try a different alphabet variant

**Problem:** Switch control not working

**Solution:**

- Verify iOS Switch Control is enabled
- Check switch connections in iOS Accessibility settings
- Ensure Dasher has "Switch" input method selected
- Test switches in iOS Switch Control first

## Tips for iPad Users

- **Split Screen**: Use Dasher in split view with other apps
- **Apple Pencil**: Adjust sensitivity for your grip
- **Landscape Mode**: Dasher works in both orientations
- **Guided Access**: Lock iPad to Dasher for dedicated use

## Tips for iPhone Users

- **Reachability**: Use one-handed mode for smaller screens
- **Touch Accommodations**: Configure in iOS accessibility if needed
- **Haptic Feedback**: Some iPhone models provide haptic feedback
