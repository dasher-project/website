# History of Dasher

## Who's Who

Dasher is brought to you by the **Inference Group**, led by
**David MacKay**, Professor in the Department of Physics and cofounder
of the information technology company Transversal.

<div class="team-grid">
  <div class="team-member">
    <h3>David MacKay</h3>
    <p class="role">Creator & Project Lead</p>
    <p>Created the first Dasher prototype in 1997. Professor of Physics and cofounder of Transversal.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="team-member">
    <h3>David Ward</h3>
    <p class="role">Lead Developer (1998-2002)</p>

## Versions of Dasher

<div class="timeline">
  <div class="timeline-item">
    <h3>Version 5.0 (2016-2024)</h3>
    <p>
      The update to version 5 was developed by Ada Majorek. Mac version updated September 2024
      to work on recent versions of macOS (10.14 Mojave through macOS 14 Sonoma).
    </p>
    <ul>
      <li>Bug fixes and improvements</li>
      <li>Feature parity with v4 for Mac</li>
      <li>Modern macOS compatibility</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <h3>Version 4.0 (October 2005)</h3>
    <p>Released for GNU/Linux and Windows.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Supports any Unicode alphabet</li>
      <li>Asian language support</li>
      <li>Button modes</li>
      <li>Game mode</li>
      <li>Automatic speed control</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <h3>Version 3.0</h3>
    <p>Released for GNU/Linux, Windows, and MacOSX.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Supports any Unicode alphabet</li>
      <li>All major languages of the world supported</li>
      <li>Open source release</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <h3>Version 2.0</h3>
    <p>For GNU/Linux and Windows desktops.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>English, upper and lower case</li>
      <li>Punctuation and numbers</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <h3>Version 1.0 (C and Tcl)</h3>
    <p>For GNU/Linux and Windows desktops.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Uses PPM as the language model</li>
      <li>Driven by mouse</li>
      <li>Several European languages and Japanese (Hiragana)</li>
      <li>English version supports capital and lower case</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <h3>Pocket PC Version</h3>
    <p>Written by David Ward. Driven by stylus on touch-screen.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Capital letters, numbers, punctuation</li>
      <li>English only</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <h3>Eye-Dasher</h3>
    <p>Written by David Ward. Driven by mouse controlled by eye-tracker.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <h3>Daishoya (JDasher)</h3>
    <p>Japanese-language version of Dasher (Hiragana), included in the C and Tcl version.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <h3>Original Prototype (Tcl)</h3>
    <p>Written by David MacKay.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Demonstrates relationship to arithmetic coding</li>
      <li>Includes crude bigram language model</li>
      <li>Runs on all platforms supporting Tcl</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>
