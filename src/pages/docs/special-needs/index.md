# Special Needs and Accessibility

<div class="intro-box">
  <p>
    Dasher is highly appropriate for computer users who are unable to use a two-handed keyboard.
    One-handed users and users with no hands love Dasher. The only ability that is required is sight.
  </p>
</div>

## Input Methods

Dasher can be driven using any two-dimensional pointing device:

<div class="input-methods">
  <div class="input-method">
    <h3>Standard Pointing Devices</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Mouse</li>
      <li>Trackpad</li>
      <li>Touchscreen</li>
      <li>Rollerball</li>
      <li>Joystick</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="input-method">
    <h3>Hands-Free Options</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Head mouse</strong> - controls cursor via head movement</li>
      <li><strong>Eye-tracker</strong> - gaze-based control for completely hands-free writing</li>
      <li><strong>Foot mouse</strong> - foot-controlled cursor</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

## Eye-Tracking Performance

With an eye-tracker, Dasher provides a completely hands-free writing system.
After one hour's practice, some users are able to write at more than **20 words per minute**.
Experienced users reach **30 words per minute**.

<div class="performance-box">
  <h3>Compared to an eye-tracker + on-screen keyboard, Dasher is:</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Faster</strong> - More efficient text entry</li>
    <li><strong>More accurate</strong> - Better error correction built in</li>
    <li><strong>More fun</strong> - Natural, fluid interaction</li>
  </ul>
</div>

For a movie demonstrating Dasher with an eye-tracker, see our
<a href="/about/#demonstrations">demonstrations page</a>.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>I am paralyzed from the shoulders down. What do I need to make Dasher work for me?</h3>

  <p><strong>If you have a PC (Linux or Windows) and can use a head-mouse:</strong></p>
  <p>
    Simply download the latest version of Dasher, and you should be able to write with Dasher.
    You may use a mouth-stick that controls the mouse coordinates as well.
  </p>

  <p><strong>If standard mice and head-mice are not an option:</strong></p>
  <p>
    You'll need an eye-tracker that can take over control of the mouse. Several systems are available
    (see recommendations below).
  </p>
</div>

## Hardware Recommendations

### Head Mice

<div class="hardware-recommendation">
  <h4>Smart-Nav Head Mouse (Highly Recommended)</h4>
  <p><strong>Manufacturer:</strong> NaturalPoint (formerly sold as TrackIR)</p>
  <p><strong>Price:</strong> About $150-$300</p>
  <p>
    Uses one reflective dot stuck to your head and a small camera attached to your Windows machine.
    We recommend this device because it is cheap, easy to set up, and versatile.
  </p>
  <p>
    <strong>Tip:</strong> You can stick the dot on your finger or toe to make a finger-mouse or toe-mouse.
    Best results are achieved by putting the dot on an extension to amplify body motion, or on the tip
    of a baseball hat.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="hardware-recommendation">
  <h4>HeadMouse Extreme (Origin Instruments)</h4>
  <p><strong>Price:</strong> About $1000</p>
  <p>
    Works instantly as a USB mouse, requiring no software at all. Works on Linux and Mac too.
    A very good product, though expensive.
  </p>
</div>

### Eye-Trackers

<div class="hardware-recommendation">
  <h4>Quickglance (Eyetech)</h4>
  <p><strong>Price:</strong> About $3000</p>
  <p>Very good value. Recommended settings: Update Rate 30, Smoothing Factor 1 or 2.</p>
</div>

<div class="hardware-recommendation">
  <h4>Tobii MyTobii</h4>
  <p><strong>Price:</strong> About $25,000</p>
  <p>
    The king of gaze-trackers - tracks both eyes and is robust to enormous head motions.
    Very impressive but expensive.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="hardware-recommendation">
  <h4>Eyegaze (LC Technologies)</h4>
  <p>A high-quality tracker, very easy to use. Works with Dasher.</p>
</div>

<div class="hardware-recommendation">
  <h4>Eye Response Erica</h4>
  <p>
    High-quality tracker with sensible patented features such as automated zoom when you dwell-click.
    Eye Response ships their product with Dasher included.
  </p>
</div>

## Speech Synthesis Integration

Dasher can be integrated with speech synthesis systems for users who need both text entry
and speech output. Integration is available for both Linux and Windows systems.
